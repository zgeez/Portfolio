Problem & goal

Can 42nd Street charge cars in motion?

Simulated SAE J2954 lanes on Midtown’s busiest crosstown artery. 42nd St averages ≈70k vehicles/day crawling at ≈3 mph: dwell-time-rich conditions for in-motion charging.

  • How much state of charge can drivers regain per urban trip?
  • Do 11 kW (WPT-3) or 22 kW (WPT-4) coils make economic sense at city scale?

RoleTeam lead on a three-member research group: scoped the study, built the simulation pipeline, and presented findings to the NYIT UREP judges.

Validation

Checked against field data

The main challenge was reconciling field-tested coil efficiencies (Electreon Gotland, Purdue I-65) with simulated NYC traffic. After tuning, the pickup curves match published data within ±5%.
